The 5-Layer Stack

Five layers. Miss one — the system breaks. Each one solo, then how they connect into a working agent on a real phone.

1

WhatsApp Cloud API

The pipe that brings customer messages from WhatsApp to your server. Not the regular WhatsApp app, not WhatsApp Business app — Meta's developer Cloud API. Free for the first 1,000 conversations / month.

Free → $30
Per month
2

Backend / Webhook

Your server. Receives the message, decides what to do, sends the reply. Vercel, Cloud Run, AWS Lambda, Railway, or Fly.io all work fine. Node.js, Python, or PHP — pick what your team knows.

$10 → $20
Per month
3

AI Brain

Claude or GPT-4. Reads the customer message, your knowledge base, and the conversation history — then decides what to answer. Critical: the brain alone is NOT your agent. Brain + Knowledge Base + your rules = agent.

$10 → $30
Per month
4

Knowledge Base

Your business: products, prices, hours, FAQ, policies. Stored in a way the AI can search instantly — embeddings + vector DB (Pinecone, Weaviate, pgvector). This is the layer that makes your agent YOURS, not generic.

$0 → $30
Per month
5

Monitoring

The dashboard. Live conversations, intervention if needed, key metrics: response time, escalations, CSAT. Tools: Retool, Bubble, custom React panel. Without this layer, you're flying blind.

$0 → $25
Per month
